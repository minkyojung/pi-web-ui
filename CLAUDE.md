## Changelog

`CHANGELOG.md` is for the person using the app. A commit that changes what
they see or what happens to their files adds one line under `[Unreleased]`,
in the same commit, in their words — not the commit's, not a file's name.
A commit that changes nothing they could notice adds nothing. When it is not
clear which, ask.
